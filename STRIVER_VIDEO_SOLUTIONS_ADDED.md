## Benefits

1. **Visual Learning**: Students can watch Striver's detailed explanations
2. **Step-by-Step Solutions**: Video walkthroughs of problem-solving approach
3. **Multiple Approaches**: Striver often covers brute force to optimal solutions
4. **Time/Space Complexity**: Clear analysis of algorithm efficiency
5. **Interview Tips**: Real interview insights and common mistakes

## Usage

When users select a problem with a video solution:
- Video icon/button appears in the problem interface
- Clicking opens Striver's YouTube video in new tab
- Students can code along while watching the explanation
