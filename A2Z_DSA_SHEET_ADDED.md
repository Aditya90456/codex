## 🚀 Usage

### Accessing the Sheet
Navigate to the DSA section and select "A2Z DSA Sheet" from the menu.

### Marking Problems as Solved
Click the checkbox next to any problem to mark it as complete. Progress is automatically saved and reflected in the topic's progress bar.

### Solving Problems
Click on any problem title to open it directly on the practice platform (LeetCode/GFG).
