## 🎓 Learning Path Recommendation

### For Beginners
1. Start with **A2Z DSA Course** (Playlist above)
2. Follow the sheet step by step
3. Watch corresponding video for each topic
4. Practice problems after each video

### For Interview Prep
1. Complete **Striver's SDE Sheet** (180 problems)
2. Focus on **Graph** and **DP** series
3. Practice company-specific problems
4. Revise patterns regularly

### For Advanced Learners
1. Deep dive into specific topics
2. Solve hard problems from each category
3. Optimize solutions
4. Teach others (best way to learn)
